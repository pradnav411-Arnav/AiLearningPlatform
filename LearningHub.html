Purpose

The Learning Hub is a page designed to introduce users to Artificial Intelligence through beginner-friendly introductory quests. It provides users with an opportunity to explore AI concepts, understand how different AI tools work, and gain hands-on experience through interactive activities. These introductory quests help users build foundational knowledge, develop confidence, and become familiar with AI before moving on to the more advanced challenges and learning paths in the Progressions page.
